# Store page

## Title

**Steal a Chibi!**

## Description

Train. Sprint. STEAL. Hatch the cutest chibis in the multiverse!

Run on your treadmill to boost your Speed, then dash into anime-inspired zones: a cherry-blossom
school, a neon ramen alley, a fox shrine forest, a ninja village, an idol concert dome, a mecha
hangar, an isekai kingdom, a demon lord's castle, the spirit realm... and the celestial heights
beyond the golden gate.

Grab a glowing egg from the nest, outrun the zone's Guardian and race it home. Your egg hatches
with an epic anime reveal into an original chibi who earns cash for you every second!

- 66 original chibis across 11 zones, plus festival, gacha and fusion exclusives
- Sizes from Tiny to TITAN, plus Silver, Golden, Rainbow and blooming mutations
- Steal from other players' bases, snatch eggs mid-run, slap them with your paper fan!
- Defend your base with a Barrier Lock and sticky ofuda traps
- Fuse three copies into an Awakened chibi, and discover secret recipe fusions
- Beat the Heavenly Gatekeeper to reach the Sacred Tree
- Rebirth for permanent boosts and legendary thief titles
- Weekly Matsuri Nights, Sakura Storms and Eclipse Hours

Everyone starts with a shield for their first 10 minutes. Have fun, and watch your base!
